Going live for real

This submission pipeline is a working simulation of App Store Connect's review flow (Submitted → In Review → Published/Changes Requested) so you can design and test the whole journey.

To actually ship to Apple's App Store or Google Play, you'll additionally need to:

  • Enroll in the Apple Developer Program ($99/yr) and/or a Google Play Developer account ($25 one-time).
  • Turn this design into a real app binary — the Export app button above gives you a working PWA to start from; wrap it with Capacitor or Cordova to build native iOS/Android binaries, or hand it to a developer as a spec.
  • Upload the signed binary via Xcode/Transporter (iOS) or the Play Console (Android), fill in the same listing details you entered here, and submit for Apple/Google's real review.
